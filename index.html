    How the model works

    Baseline: the most recent 12 months of clean data, scaled to a full year if a month is missing or excluded.
    Discharges in year t = baseline × (1 + population growth)t × (1 + use rate change)t × (1 + market share change × t/10) × (1 − outpatient shift × t/10)
    ALOS moves in a straight line from today's value to the year 10 target. Patient days = discharges × ALOS. Average daily census = patient days ÷ 365. Beds needed = census ÷ target occupancy.
    Data checks flag duplicate or missing months, zero or negative values, ALOS outside 2.5 to 8 days, and occupancy above 100%.
